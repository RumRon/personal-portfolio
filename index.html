About Me

I am Rui Jiang, a PhD student in Urban and Regional Planning at the University of Florida, interested in spatial analytics, AI, sustainability, and urban systems.

I am affiliated with ReCityX Lab at UF, directed by Dr. Yan Wang. The lab focuses on resilience, regeneration, and responsive cities.

This site shares my academic background, completed work, and personal interests. Ongoing PhD research will not be posted before it is ready for public release.
